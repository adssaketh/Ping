## Findings that change the master prompt
1. Spring conversion needs `stiffness / mass` as well (not only the damping ratio). Documented in `SpringConversion`.
2. The "glow blobs under the bottom bar" do not exist in code; they are the glass bar's blur + saturate over avatars behind it (design-tokens section 7). Nothing to port.
3. A new workflow resets GITHUB_RUN_NUMBER; versionCode offset 1000 prevents "cannot install over".
4. Haze 2.1 (released 2 Nov 2026, Apache-2.0, active) is split into `haze` + `haze-blur`; API differs from the 1.x/2.0 docs. Source read from the sources jars.
5. Category chips use Tailwind v4 OKLCH colours, not hex (Phase 3 converts them exactly).
6. `package.json` 2.2.0 / build.gradle `2.2`; Settings footer shows "Version 2.2 (build 52)".

## How to measure glass on your phone (Ping Dev, release build)
Install `ping-dev-release-TESTKEY.apk` (signed with a throwaway key, installs next to Ping, cannot send or receive SMS). Open "Glass bar prototype", tap the gear button, then "Run 10 s scroll test" once per setting:
Solid; Glass strong 62 %; Glass medium 62 %; Glass soft 62 %; Glass strong 95 %; edge off. Use "Copy all results" and paste them to me.
Read: p95/p99 under the budget (16.7 ms at 60 Hz, 8.3 ms at 120 Hz) and a low "over budget" percentage = steady. Don't use the debug APK for numbers.
